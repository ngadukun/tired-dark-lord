## What it is

A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very
tired. He never says so — the tiredness lives in the voice instead: the
sighing stairs, the destiny speech he rehearses less and less, the prophecy
that forgot to mention breakfast, volcano rent tripling, and his minions
unionising over dental. His scheme, in the end, is tea. The trap is the
kettle clicking.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22+) writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` and prints
it, with its word and character count:

```sh
node main.ts
```

The monologue is exactly 100 words and 540 characters — inside the gate of
1–600 characters.
